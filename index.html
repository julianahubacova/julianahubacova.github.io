SBA Loan Default Prediction

A credit-risk model that flags loans likely to default, at 0.98 ROC-AUC on a public SBA lending dataset. Every prediction ships with a SHAP explanation, so there's a reason behind the score and not just a number. I'm packaging it as a FastAPI service so it can be queried live.

SMB Loan Data Quality Pipeline

A Bronze / Silver / Gold pipeline that runs ten automated quality checks and logs every failure to a Delta table. Bad records get caught before they reach a dashboard, instead of after someone has already acted on them. Results land in Power BI.

The Birthday Paradox

How many people do you need in a room before two share a birthday? Far fewer than it feels — 23 already gets you to a coin flip. This tool lets you play with the numbers and watches a Monte Carlo simulation land on the same answer the formula predicts.
