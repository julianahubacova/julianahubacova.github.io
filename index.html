Hello, I'm Juliana Hubacova

Data Scientist | Data Analyst

I am a data-driven strategist passionate about using AI, data science, and analytics to drive real-world impact. With a Master’s in Analytics from McGill University and a strong foundation in Economics and Mathematics, I specialize in predictive modeling, AI-powered decision-making, and business optimization.

Throughout my journey, I’ve worked on projects that bridge the gap between data science and business strategy. From developing a data lake for real-time project monitoring at AtkinsRéalis to building AI-powered product recommendations for ALDO, I thrive in environments that require both technical depth and strategic foresight.

My experience spans machine learning, financial analytics, and deep learning applications—whether it’s forecasting stock prices, detecting fake reviews using NLP, or optimizing power plant operations. I also enjoy solving complex challenges, like analyzing earnings management models with SAS or leveraging YOLOv8 for face and emotion detection.

Beyond data, I have a strong leadership background. As Vice-President of UN Women University of Montreal, I worked on global gender equality initiatives, representing the association at CSW66 and CSW67 in New York. I also competed in the BRSM x ALDO Retail Gen AI Hackathon, where my team developed an AI-powered shopping assistant, winning the AWS Tech Use Prize.

When I’m not immersed in analytics, you’ll find me competing in swimming, running 10Ks for charity, or strategizing on the tennis court. With a passion for continuous learning and innovation, I’m always exploring new ways to turn data into action.
